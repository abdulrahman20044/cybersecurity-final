About Cybersecurity

Understanding digital threats and learning how to protect our connected world.

What Is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, and data from digital attacks and unauthorized access.

Common Cyber Threats

Phishing

Fake emails and websites designed to steal passwords and personal information.

Malware

Malicious software that can damage devices or steal sensitive data.

Ransomware

Malware that encrypts files and demands payment to restore access.

Social Engineering

Manipulating people into revealing confidential information.

How Can We Stay Safe?

Use Strong Passwords

Create long and unique passwords for every online account.

Enable MFA

Multi-factor authentication adds an extra layer of protection.

Update Your Devices

Install security updates to fix known vulnerabilities.

Our Mission

CyberShield aims to spread cybersecurity awareness and help people understand how to protect their digital lives.
